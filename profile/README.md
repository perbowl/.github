## What this is

PerBowl traces pet food brands to the companies named in state registrations, and those companies to their licensed
plants and FDA inspection records. It also tracks every dog and cat food recall the US FDA has classified, with free
same-day recall alerts.

## How the records are checked

- **Public records first.** Maker matches come from state registrations, licences and FDA records, and each page links its sources.
- **Doubtful matches are held back, not guessed.**
- **Labels are read by hand** where a maker's site forbids automated reading, with screenshots kept as evidence on each food's page.
- **Disagreements are shown.** When a maker's own pages contradict each other, PerBowl shows both and says which one it uses.
- **Mistakes are fixed in public** on the [corrections page](https://perbowl.com/corrections/).
- **Records, not advice.** PerBowl is not run by a veterinarian and does not give feeding or medical advice.

The full method is on [Methodology](https://perbowl.com/methodology/) and [How we read food labels](https://perbowl.com/how-we-read-food-labels/).
How the site is paid for: [How we make money](https://perbowl.com/how-we-make-money/).
